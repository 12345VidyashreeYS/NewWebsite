Where our Pipes are used

RCC Hume Pipes are widely used in a variety of construction and infrastructure projects.

Road Drainage
Used to carry rainwater away from roads and
help maintain proper drainage systems.
Culvert Construction
Used in suitable culvert projects to allow water to pass beneath roads, railways and access routes.
Stormwater Management
Used in systems for collecting and directing rainwater, helping to manage water flow in urban and rural areas.
Infrastructure Projects
Used in drainage and other civil engineering projects such as residential layouts, industrial areas, commercial developments and more.
